Double0skys Fortune Telling Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026

At a glance

  • No account, no name, no email address needed to use the app.
  • No ads, no analytics, and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • Your birth details are saved on your phone. They are sent out only to write a reading you asked for.
  • Readings are written by AI (Claude, made by Anthropic).
  • Purchases go through Google Play and RevenueCat. We never see your card details.
  • We don't sell your information.

Who we are

Double0skys Fortune Telling (“the app”) is a tarot and astrology app made by Double0skys, an independent developer. “We” and “us” in this policy mean Double0skys. You can reach us at the email address at the end of this page.

What the app keeps on your phone

  • A random app ID, created the first time you open the app. It isn't linked to your name, email, or phone number.
  • The birth details you enter: birthday, and optionally birth time and birth place.
  • Your daily card, daily horoscope, weekly forecast, and birth chart reading, so you can read them again without waiting.

This stays on your phone until you change it or delete the app's data (for example by uninstalling the app).

Our reading server

Our server writes your reading and sends it back to the app. It does not save your questions, birth details, or readings in a database. It keeps only:

  • a count of readings per app ID for the current day, used for fair-use limits and cleared regularly;
  • general daily readings shared by everyone, such as the horoscope for each star sign and the message for each card of the day, which contain nothing personal;
  • basic technical logs, such as when a request was made and whether it worked, for fixing problems.

Connections between the app and our server are encrypted (HTTPS).

AI-written readings

Readings are written by Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic. To write a reading, our server sends Anthropic the cards drawn and, depending on the reading, your question and birth details. Anthropic processes this under its commercial terms and privacy policy, and by default does not use it to train its models.

Readings are for reflection, inspiration, and entertainment. They are not medical, legal, financial, or other professional advice. Please don't include sensitive personal information, such as health details or other people's names, in your questions.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Google Play. We never see your card or bank details. We use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions and one-time purchases. RevenueCat receives your random app ID and your purchase records (which items, when, and their status), and our server asks RevenueCat whether a reading has been paid for. See the Google privacy policy and the RevenueCat privacy policy. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in the Google Play Store app.

What we don't do

  • We don't ask for your name, email address, phone number, or contacts.
  • We don't use your device's location. The birth place is only what you type.
  • We don't show ads or use analytics or advertising trackers.
  • We don't sell your information or share it for advertising.

Your choices

  • You can change your birth details at any time on the Stars tab, and clear the optional birth time and place.
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in your phone's settings, deletes everything the app saved on your phone.
  • Because our server doesn't store your questions or birth details, there is nothing personal there to delete. To have your purchase records deleted, see Delete my data.

Delete my data

You can ask us to delete your data at any time. Email us at double0skysthegreat@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my data”. We will delete your data within 30 days of receiving your request and email you when it's done.

The app has no accounts, so please include your Google Play order number (it starts with “GPA.” and is on the purchase receipt Google emailed you). We use it to find and delete your purchase records held by RevenueCat. If you never bought anything, there is no data about you outside your phone; deleting the app removes everything it saved there.

Google keeps its own record of payments made through Google Play, under the Google privacy policy. You can manage that from your Google account.

Children

The app is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has used the app and want us to help, contact us.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
